# 8 · Douyin science: new-film experiments toward a million views

**English** · [简体中文](zh-CN/08-douyin-science-growth.md)

Date: **8 October 2026**. AI History, entropy and neutrinos were practice projects. This plan concerns **new high-quality science films**, retaining the working Opus 5.5 coded-animation pipeline. Science remains first, cute cats second and AI drama third. No new film was produced or published in this round.

Assume the target means **one million organically recommended views**. It is an outcome, not a guaranteed workflow result. Separate paid and organic views. Durations, pacing and batch sizes below are suggestions, not official Douyin thresholds.

## Official evidence and limits

- [Douyin’s recommendation explanation](https://trust.douyin.com/algorithm/) and [technical account](https://trust.douyin.com/article/15358?channel=home&enter_from=channel_page) describe interests, content features and multiple behavioral signals; completion alone is insufficient.
- The [Safety and Trust Center](https://trust.douyin.com/) encourages originality, useful discoveries and surprise; these do not promise distribution.
- A [Douyin Creator Assistant lesson](https://www.douyin.com/video/7506120433238822181) discusses two-second exits and comparison with similar work. Its AI-generated page summary was not checked against the audio frame by frame; do not infer algorithm weights or universal pass marks. Use fields actually available in the account.

There is no basis here for fixed initial traffic pools, guaranteed completion-rate triggers or a magic publishing hour. Practice-film counts of 208/653/224 are snapshots, not a diagnosis for new films.

## Content proposition for new films

Design initially for **non-specialists curious about everyday science**, an audience hypothesis to test. Each film answers one clear question: show a familiar but surprising phenomenon, reveal its hidden process with animation, and leave the viewer able to explain the answer.

Choose questions with these properties:

1. Understandable without specialist terms.
2. A visible phenomenon with conditions or before/after comparison.
3. Animation reveals something filming cannot easily show, rather than serving as decoration.
4. One main mechanism is enough; no complete disciplinary history required.
5. Reliable scientific sources, without invented numbers, fear or false exaggeration.

Broad appeal and attractive imagery are hypotheses, not proof of million-view potential. Make three to five new question-led films to establish a test baseline.

## Initial topic drafts

Ordering reflects explainability, everyday relevance and coded-animation value, **not view predictions**. Sources verify science; they do not automatically authorize copying their images or videos.

| Order | Working title | Audience entry and visual explanation | Scientific checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Why does a magnet fall through copper as if in slow motion?** | Show slow descent, then reveal currents and fields in a cutaway pipe | Eddy currents/Lenz’s law; do not call copper entirely non-magnetic, show permanent hovering or invent descent times. [IOP demonstration](https://spark.iop.org/electromagnetic-braking-copper-pipe), [UBC demonstration](https://democart.phas.ubc.ca/doku.php?id=demonstrations%3A5_electricity_and_magnetism%3A5k_electromagnetic_induction%3Amagnets_through_copper_tube%3Astart) |
| 2 | **Same temperature: why does metal feel colder?** | Matching thermometers but different sensation; heat flow, skin cooling, then reversal above hand temperature | Preserve equal-temperature/below-hand-temperature conditions; sensation is not an object thermometer. [University of Illinois](https://van.physics.illinois.edu/ask/listing/256977) |
| 3 | **Where in the sky is a rainbow?** | Move the observer and trace rays from different droplets to the eye | Sun/drop/eye geometry, refraction/reflection/dispersion and color order. [NASA observer explanation](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2013-march-27-a-horizon-rainbow-in-paris/), [formation explanation](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2010-august-7-rainbow-at-sunset/) |
| 4 | **One plate of food: why are some bites hot and others cold?** | Food heat map, spatial field distribution and turntable accumulation | Fields, food properties and heat transfer all matter; label simplified diagrams, do not claim uniform inside-out heating. [TU Delft](https://interactivetextbooks.tudelft.nl/showthephysics/demos/demo49/demo49.html) |
| 5 | **Why is a spinning top harder to tip over?** | Still/spinning comparison with gravity retained, torque and angular-momentum direction change | Precession/approximations; gravity does not disappear, and external torque changes angular momentum. [MIT course](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/16-07-dynamics-fall-2009/resources/mit16_07f09_lec30/) |

Start with topic one; topics two and three are subsequent candidates. The film must deliver its title’s promise. Finalize titles with the storyboard.

## First-film structure: magnet and copper

Try a 60–90-second question film first, extending it if accurate explanation needs more time. These timings are a storyboard draft.

| Approximate time | Visual and narrative task |
|---|---|
| 0–3 seconds | Show the descent contrast and ask why it looks slowed; begin with the phenomenon |
| 3–12 seconds | Identify electromagnetic braking and comparison conditions; label original animation as a principle illustration, not measurement |
| 12–40 seconds | Reveal motion, changing flux, current and induced field in a transparent pipe, adding one explanatory layer at a time |
| 40–65 seconds | Connect invisible forces to slowing in the full scene; verify directions, forces and terminology |
| 65–90 seconds | Answer plainly, then offer one related application or verifiable extension, preserving necessary conditions |

Scientific foundations are the IOP/UBC sources above. Motion, cuts and layers should improve understanding; frequent mechanical cutting is unnecessary. Cover and opening promise the same answer. Inventor Cat may be a small identifier/presenter, leaving primary space to the explanation.

## Production and publishing experiment

1. **Validate question and opening first.** Draft two openings and inexpensive animated previews. Check whether non-specialists understand the question. Qualitative feedback is not a platform A/B test.
2. **Finish science and audiovisual delivery.** Use Opus 5.5 for source organization, storyboard, code, sound and captions; verify calculations, arrows, units and model assumptions. Review contact sheets, full sound playback and phone-size readability.
3. **Publish new films individually.** Make three to five at a quality-preserving pace. Choose framing for the explanation; re-layout vertical editions instead of damaging diagrams through cropping. Match title, cover and body.
4. **Log consistent windows.** Suggested observations at 24 hours, 72 hours and seven days are a recording schedule, not recommendation cycles. Record available analytics and mark missing fields unavailable.
5. **Change the next film from evidence.** Change one primary factor and log the hypothesis. Different films, dates and audiences are not randomized experiments; one view difference does not prove causation.

| New film | Topic/duration | Publication/window | Views | Early exit/retention | Average watch/completion | Shares/saves/new followers | Main variable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Magnet/pipe | Draft | Unpublished | Untested | Untested | Untested | Untested | Phenomenon opening/layered diagram |
| Equal-temperature metal | Draft | Unpublished | Untested | Untested | Untested | Untested | Bodily-experience entry |
| Rainbow location | Draft | Unpublished | Untested | Untested | Untested | Untested | Observer perspective/ray paths |

Possible interpretations, not proven diagnoses:

- Early exits: check first frame, clarity of the question and prerequisite vocabulary.
- Continued middle losses: check repetition, missing reasoning steps, density and explanatory diagrams.
- Completion with little sharing/following: check whether the answer is useful to retell and the series proposition is clear.
- Relatively good watching/engagement with low views: accumulate comparable films and inspect time/sample differences; neither suppression nor later expansion is established.

When a pattern performs relatively better, create related new questions and test repeatability. Organic million-view reach may or may not occur. The first production experiment should deliver new completed films, traceable science and data useful for the next decision.
