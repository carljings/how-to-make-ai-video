# Credits

An original film. No reference video, footage, photographs, illustrations or audio samples were used.

- Picture: JavaScript on an HTML canvas, rendered frame by frame in headless Chrome.
- Music and sound: synthesized with the Web Audio API in `src/score.js`; the melodies come from the seeds' angles.
- Written, directed and coded with Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code.

## Sources for the science

- H. Vogel, "A better way to construct the sunflower head", *Mathematical Biosciences* 44 (1979): the seed-placement model used for every flower.
- S. Douady & Y. Couder, "Phyllotaxis as a physical self-organized growth process", *Physical Review Letters* 68 (1992), and *Journal of Theoretical Biology* 178 (1996): the repulsion model behind Act V, simulated in `tools/emerge.mjs`.
- The golden angle 360° ÷ φ² and the continued fraction of φ = [1; 1, 1, …] are standard results of number theory.
