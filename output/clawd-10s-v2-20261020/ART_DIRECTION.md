## Direction: "一拍推倒一块" (each beat knocks down a domino)

**The rule (0–1.36 s).**
- Tiny Clawd stands on the first domino of a small ivory chain at dusk, on a dark mirror plain.
- A whole plain of black dominos stands set up around, visibly waiting (a reverse angle, with the question on clean sky).
- On each eighth-note onset of the thin bar (6 measured onsets), the next domino falls, and Clawd hops along the tops, one hop per beat.

**The gap (1.36–1.88 s).** The song leaves out an eighth note, and the chain also leaves a gap. The last domino falls flat short of the next one. Clawd rides it down, looks at the gap and then at you: "下一下呢？"

**The pickup (1.884 s).** On the measured hit, Clawd body-checks the next domino.

**The build (2.15–4.28 s).** Each build onset (11 measured) topples a bigger domino along a snaking line that darkens from ivory to black. The last one tips the black monolith, which falls across the view.

**The drop (4.281 s).**
- The monolith lands on the drop. The slam sends a shock hop through the plain, and dusk turns to night.
- The whole plain falls outward in a front whose speed follows the song's measured low end. Five spiral arms race ahead.
- Every fallen domino's pips light up and breathe with the bass. The world itself becomes the light.
- The front reaches Clawd on the drop's third measured beat (5.343 s), about a second after the slam. Clawd jumps and spins on it, and dances on each beat. Each beat sends a ring of light through the fallen field.

**The landing (8.54–10 s).** On the last bar line, Clawd strikes a pose and waves over a glowing sea of fallen dominos: "明知道要来，还是忍不住等它".

**Why this direction.** A domino line *is* prediction made visible: you can see what is going to fall. The withheld eighth and the growing line make the wait physical, and the drop pays it off at a scale far beyond the character.

**Directions rejected.**
- An ocean that draws back before a tsunami wave: beautiful, but a convincing breaking wave is a large rendering risk for one preview.
- A marble or pinball run: too small-scale for a phone.

**Clawd.**
- Smaller and cuter: 0.8 units tall next to 1.0–1.45 domino units and a monolith 9 units tall.
- Most shots keep it at about 10–15% of frame height. There is one justified close-up in the gap, and the wide reveal briefly goes to about 7%.
- Soft rounded vinyl. The silhouette is measured: body 8×6, black 1×1 eyes with a tiny catch-light, 2×2 arms and four legs.
- It always turns to the lens. It hops, squashes, rides, body-checks, braces, spins and waves.

**Look.**
- A dusk gradient sky with the sun just off frame right as a rim light, and long shadows on a dark polished plain that reflects the sky.
- Ivory rule dominos against black dominos with ivory pips.
- After the drop, night falls and the fallen field's warm pips and faces are the light.
- Restrained bloom; a shock-ring lens distortion only on the slam. No white flashes, no decorative particles, light grain.

**Science framing.** The captions only pose the question and describe the experience the demo stages: you could see it coming and still waited for it. That framing is illustrative, not a brain measurement. There is no IQ, dopamine number, addiction law or "best predictability ratio".
