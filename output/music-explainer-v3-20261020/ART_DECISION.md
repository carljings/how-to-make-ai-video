# ART_DECISION: v3 r2 「声音长廊」 (10 s preview), by Opus 5.5 (sole owner of the visuals)

**Why r2 replaces the prediction track.** The track was truthful but read as a generic neon rhythm game: small, low-contrast elements, a long run of identical rings and a modest drop. r2 keeps the measured core (a beat grid fitted to the first six attacks; one missing attack; the drop landing on the grid) and gives it a bold, editorial image.

**The image.**
- A thick horizontal strip of the song's real loudness, with rounded bars whose orange base is the measured bass, scrolls past a "现在" playhead.
- **Left of 现在 is heard (实测). Right of it is only expected (示意):** hollow rings on the grid. When a real attack arrives, a beam reaches up from the bar and fills its ring.
- One ring arrives empty and stays cyan and hollow: the missing attack.
- During the build, the bar-level ring swells as the expected big beat approaches.
- Small Clawd stands on the floor under the playhead, looking up at the sound. It bobs on attacks, startles at the miss, braces in the build, is launched by the drop, dances on the beat and waves at the end.

**The drop.**
1. A gold shock ring and a lens ripple fire from the met ring.
2. The flat diagram swings into a low 3/4 view: the drop's bars become a corridor of towers whose bass bases glow.
3. A confirmation wave runs back along every met ring, and the expected rings ahead warm to gold.

**The landing.** The timeline zooms out until the whole 10 s record fits. Under the takeaway "上头的原因之一：听出规律 → 预期 → 等到", brackets name the three parts of the record: 规律 (the first six), 少一下 (the cyan ring) and 等到 (the drop).

**Typography.** One headline at a time over a dark top scrim, with one orange accent word. Short callouts in pills with leader lines appear one at a time, and every measured element says 实测.

**Rejected.**
- A brain or auditory-pathway fly-through: it risks anatomy that explains nothing in 10 s.
- Another toy world (dominoes): an analogy with no bridge to the mechanism.
