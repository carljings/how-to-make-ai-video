# Roadster v4: 「光切」 (Light-Sheet) — creative direction

Director: Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`, effort max), under the human's delegated art direction. This is a revision of the approved v3 storyboard. The narration (16 lines, 155 characters, 37.68 s measured), the section order, the factual boundaries and the two-demo separation are unchanged. Timed shot list: `storyboard-v4.zh-CN.md`. Decisions, limits and next actions: `production-log.md`.

## 1. What the human rejected, read from the actual frames

The human's verdict: "第一个坡和视频里的不太一样，最上面的没有平的" and "不够酷炫，要有科技风格，抓人眼球".

1. **The ramp is the wrong shape.**
   - v3 is three arcs that stop at 145°.
   - In the source, the curl carries on to a **straight upper deck** that runs back over the approach. In the elevated wide (31–44 s) it is a level line hung from a gantry truss. In the main wide (8.0 s) it is a long straight band whose two edges are dead straight.
   - So the car drives **inverted along a ceiling** before it leaves the free end. That is the most impossible-looking part of the real demo, and v3 omits it.
2. **Frame 0 shows no impossibility.**
   - It is a big glossy close-up of the car on a featureless grey strip, with no height, no void and no gravity cue.
   - The giant white 比墙还陡 covers the lower 40 %.
   - It reads as a toy car on a tilted board.
3. **The look is flat.**
   - A grey void, no atmosphere and no light design.
   - The "technology" lives only in thin vector arrows pasted on top.
   - Stills cut to stills; the pushes are barely perceptible; the first 5 s have no kinetic energy.

## 2. Concept: we cut the air open with light

The film gets one instrument and keeps it: a **laser light-sheet**. This is the real tool that wind tunnels and flow labs (PIV) use to make invisible air visible.

- Where the sheet crosses a surface, it draws a razor-thin line of cold white light.
- Where it crosses air, it lights the air in that plane.
- The sheet is how the film "thinks". It traces the ramp's true profile, including the flat ceiling, the moment it appears. It is the plane of every cross-section, and its particles are the air pressure that holds the car.
- It replaces random tech decoration. No HUD, no numbers, no confetti: every glowing line is a real intersection with real geometry.

**Palette.** The palette is v3's meaning code, pushed brighter:

|---|---|
| Arena | black |
| Source ramp neon | crimson |
| Sheet (the instrument) | ice white, `#EAF6FF` |

| Gravity | white |

## 4. The rest of the film (milestone 2; order and voice as approved)

- **Press (P1/P2, 5.5–15.0).** We are inside the sheet. The car's centre section and the ramp profile glow as sheet lines. Gold PIV particles fill the plane: dense outside, emptied in the gap as the fans pull. The low-pressure lens follows, then ticks merge into the press arrow, then 吸→压.
- **Tyre (15.0–17.8).** The sheet moves to the near wheel plane. The tyre's contour sits on the concrete line, the contact patch flares, and traction arrows run along the ceiling.
- **Chronophotograph (17.8–23.2).** The full ramp now includes the ceiling. On the ceiling ghosts, the press arrow points **straight up** into the deck while gravity points straight down. 哪怕是往上 becomes literal, and stronger than v3's slanted arrow.
- **Lip (23.2–28.6).** The free end of the ceiling. The gap opens from the nose, the lens shrinks, the press shortens progressively, and the first observed jet fires (relay).
- **X-ray (28.6–32.0).** The sheet becomes the x-ray scan line sweeping nose to tail; schematic vessels.
- **Jets (32.0–36.9).** Replay-B angle. The car rolls upright from the inverted departure, then the plumes go down and the push goes up, into the cloud.
- **Kickflip (37.1–46.5).** v2 plates and overlays, separated by black and 另一场演示, unchanged.
- **Ending (46.7–58.3).** The poster with the corrected ramp: warm press arrows on the wall and ceiling, cold jets in the air, and the sheet line tracing the boundary between borrowed and spent.

## 5. Corrected ramp: what is observed and what is inferred

- **Observed** in native frames:
  - In the elevated wide (31–44 s) the curl ends in a long straight deck. It runs back over the approach, parallel to a horizontal gantry chord above it, and is seen edge-on as one thin line (both neon edges overlap).
  - In the main wide (8.0 s) the upper band's two edges are straight to within about 1 px over about 200 px, and the band widens toward its free end because the deck comes toward the camera.
  - In replay B (18 s) the car is seen roof-first from below at the deck's free end.
- **Inferred** by a small visually checked fit: deck angle, deck length and curl radius.
  - Two views were used, with a camera-only solve per candidate.
  - The lower transition is v3's.
  - Scale comes only from the slab-width estimate (3.05 m, against the car).
  - Every number is a fit, not a Tesla dimension. **No dimension appears on screen.**
- **Perspective.** The deck's slant in the main wide and its "flat line" in the elevated wide are the same horizontal deck seen from a ground camera and from a camera near deck height. This is why the old 145° reading was wrong.

## 6. Engineering rules kept

- Every pose, contact and overlay is a pure function of time (`v3_ramp` → `v3_film` → `render_v3` / `export_v3_overlay`). There is no state and no randomness.
- Geometry checks:
  - wheels on the surface within 4 mm;
  - body clearance of at least 15 mm on the wall, curl and ceiling;
  - no body intersection after the lip.
- Gravity is always world down. The press is always along the local surface normal into the ramp. Kickflip directions are unchanged.
- The airborne departure is re-derived for an inverted car leaving a level deck. The roll toward upright is fitted, and no flight claim is added.
- Cables stay visible in the kickflip. There is no clean take-off or touchdown claim.
- Internal v3 module names stay, to avoid a pointless rename. Outputs go to new `v4-*` folders.

## 7. Decisions after the actual proofs (proof01, proof02; Opus 5.5)

- **The air is invisible until the sheet cuts it.** proof01's world haze made every light a milky veil, with froxel checker. The opening now has no haze. The only lit air is the sheet's own faint veil, and the gold particles in the cross-sections. This replaces "haze lit by floods from below".
- **The sheet in the source wide sweeps across the ramp's width.** It ignites beyond the far edge at 4.58 s, crosses the car at 4.90 and locks at 5.06. The lock plane is y = −0.42 m, through the near half of the car, not the centre plane: the painted centre line sits at y = 0, and a sheet line on top of it reads as a lane stripe. The line also cuts the car's paint.
- **Inside the body the camera looks straight through the sheet.** The press, tyre, lip and x-ray views are side-on to the plane, so a surface line would be seen edge-on. The intersections are therefore drawn as projected razor lines in the sheet plane y = 0, the plane that already holds the air, ticks and low-pressure lens:
  - press, lip and x-ray: the ramp profile and the car's centre section;
  - tyre: the plane slides to the near wheel (surface line and tyre ring);
  - x-ray: the scan bar keeps the same ice white.
- **Ending.** The sheet stands edge-on at the deck's free end, drawn as the car leaves on 腾了空 (50.87 s). It is the boundary between borrowed air (the ramp ghosts, right) and carried gas (the air ghosts, left).
- **Car light under the deck.** Wherever the car hangs under the deck, the hero rig's key and rims sit in the open void below it. Camera-up placement put them above the slab, which shadowed them.
- **Poster.** It is refitted to the corrected departure: the landed car and its cloud are inside the frame, and the ramp crop holds the free end and the truss.
- **Slates** name the device: 光切剖面 and 光切扫描. They are still labelled 原理示意 and 非实拍. The light-sheet is an explanatory CG device, not a claim about Tesla's instruments.
