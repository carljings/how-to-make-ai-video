# LEARNED — v9 「空气现形 · 翻身」 (Opus 5.5)

These are working principles drawn from this film's revisions, judged from stills and measurements. Nobody has watched
the film in motion, listened to it, or reported audience results. No retention numbers are claimed.

## Payoff and structure
1. **The source's best moment is the payoff. Don't cut away from it to explain it.** v8 put a 3.5 s diagram in the
   middle of the departure, which hid the most spectacular thing in the source. In v9 the departure plays whole, and
   the explanation follows as an answer (the air-vision replay, then the gas plate). Show the event, then explain it.
2. **The WOW came from the reference event, not from added effects.** One unbroken shot, the lip kept as a fixed
   reference, the car rolling toward us, and the floor and cloud revealed as it drops: the extra scale came from
   staging the reference event as a fitted reconstruction. No flashes, booms or particles were added.
3. **Tension and release through time and stillness.** The camera is locked at the edge. The speed slows to about
   ¼ through the roll, then returns to about real time on the plunge. The camera follows, settles as the jets catch,
   then tilts down for the reveal. The camera motion follows the beats of the event.
4. **Slow motion must be labelled.** 「CG重建 · 慢放」 lets the shot be slow and dramatic without implying real flight
   time.
5. **Type can act too.** 「吸附减弱」 falls away as the car tips off the lip. One line of text does its job and then
   physically clears the frame for the image.
6. **Sparse sound tied to visible states.** The pad drains at the lip. The question motif's rising notes land one per
   visible surface, and the catch is a warm chord that does not resolve. Home is kept back for the final answer.
   Everything stays quiet and additive, with no noise source.

## Matching a source pose
7. **Check visibility from the source camera before retiming.** Two rounds treated "the CG keeps a broad canopy"
   as a timing lag. The cause was the roll *direction*: both source cameras sit on one side, and the old sense kept
   the roof facing them until ~120°. Compute which surface faces a known eye (dot products of car axes with the
   view direction) before touching keys. One sign change fixed what retiming could not.
8. **From the same eye, compare visible-face ORDER, not 2D angles or pixel silhouettes.** For a pinhole camera the eye
   position fixes the viewing rays, and so which faces of the same geometry are visible and which contours they form.
   The image-space silhouette also depends on orientation, intrinsics/FOV and framing, so a shared eye does not give
   an identical 2D projection. Shooting the WOW from the audited source eye made the face-order comparison fair. It
   is geometrically comparable only up to ~9.25–9.75; after that the source camera follows the car and is not
   calibrated, so later pairs show order and lip relation only.
9. **Reject measurements that break physics.** A fixed-eye reconstruction that implied a drop faster than free fall
   was discarded, not fitted.
10. **When evidence conflicts, correct partially and say so.** Nose and tail landmarks disagreed on the climb lag, so
    half of the lag was applied and the limit was documented. No single precise timewarp was claimed.
11. **Clearance is a whole-mesh question.** A sparse self-test said 78 mm; Codex's triangle test said 15 mm. Use the
    stricter method, and reproduce it before relying on it.

## Light and exposure
12. **Light the surfaces the story turns on.** Bright lamps don't explain orientation. A wash under the flight path made
    the underside reveal readable while keeping it dark.
13. **Proxy thresholds serve the picture.** A number I set myself (~70/255) flagged a borderline case. The frame then
    answered the real question (dark, patterned, legible, below the paint, deck and plumes), and that decided it. Write
    down that a judgement call was made rather than presenting the number as a pass.
14. **Fix luminance pops at their cause.** The handover jump came from a light-slot swap passing through zero. A wider
    swap, and holding the other light steady through it, removed it.

## Collaboration
15. **Lead with decisions and precise orders.** Exact frames, pass criteria and stop conditions let Codex return real
    native pixels quickly. Its neutral reporting of the persistent canopy mismatch is what exposed the sign error.
16. **Never treat diagnostics as proof.** SwiftShader stills guided direction. Only native and encoded frames decided,
    and even those are stills, not motion or sound.
