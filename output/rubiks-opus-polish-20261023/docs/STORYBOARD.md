# STORYBOARD — 《2.51 秒的世界纪录，为什么没拿冠军？》

1080×1920 · 30 fps · ≈ 158 s · Canvas 2D · original score at 120 BPM (1 beat = 0.5 s, 1 bar = 2 s); every cut
below sits on a beat. Headline = the big top-left text (the explanation). Footnote = small grey line above the
Douyin caption area. Every fact maps to `FACTS.md`.

What changes from Codex v2: one strong action per idea instead of a centred cube on every shot; the camera moves
(macro, explode, pull-back to a universe, flat diagrams, split-flap boards); the act light changes colour; a real
score with a drop; the hook is answered with a fact Codex never used (the record solve is the one the rules drop).
