Deep space. The kitten floats weightless among planets made of giant balls of yarn: a big pink yarn planet in the foreground right with loose strands of thread drifting from it, a teal yarn planet with a ring made of a loose strand of mustard-yellow yarn, a small mustard yarn moon, and a knitting needle drifting like a space station. A purple and blue nebula, sparkling stars, glitter-like star dust. The kitten floats with its legs splayed and tail curling, reaching out one white paw to tap the pink yarn planet, eyes wide and playful. The glowing blue morpho butterfly glides nearby with a trail of light. Soft rim light from the nebula, magenta and blue tones.
