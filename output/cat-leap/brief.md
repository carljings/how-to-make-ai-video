## Story in three lines

1. Beginning: dusk, a ginger kitten on the windowsill locks eyes on a glowing blue butterfly — pupils go huge, bottom wiggles — and it pounces.
2. Middle: the pounce never lands. On every downbeat the kitten bounds into a new world: sunset clouds (its blanket), koi swimming through a lantern sky (its fishbowl and string lights), a milk wave on a cookie surfboard (its saucer), inside a snow globe, a galaxy of yarn planets, and a leap across the moon (its paper lamp) where it finally catches the butterfly.
3. End: it wakes with a jolt in its basket in that same room; the real butterfly lands on its nose; it goes cross-eyed and sneezes; the butterfly drifts off, the bottom starts to wiggle again — and the film loops.
