# Storyboard: 一跃 · One Leap

120 BPM, 24 fps: one beat = 0.5 s = 12 frames, one bar = 2 s. Every cut lands on a downbeat. 36 s = 18 bars.
Clips: Seedance 2.5, 720p, image-to-video from a GPT Image key frame (first frame), native sound effects only (no generated music).

## The kitten (locked before any footage)

橘子 (Juzi): a ten-week-old ginger tabby kitten, real fur, white chest and four white socks, a white blaze down the nose, big round amber eyes, pink nose, a thin red collar with a small gold bell. The butterfly: a blue morpho whose wings glow softly and leave a faint trail of light dust (only in the dream; the real one at the end does not glow).

The room (seen in the hook and the ending, so a second viewing reveals where each dream world came from): a cosy attic room at dusk, a round paper lamp, warm string lights in the window, a goldfish bowl, a saucer of milk with a cookie, a snow globe, a basket of yarn, a fluffy white blanket in a wicker cat basket.

## Charm check (free, before any footage)

- Someone to care about: one kitten, in every shot, wanting one thing (the butterfly). ✓
- Funny or cute beats: bottom wiggle, wobbling on the cookie, batting snow, cross-eyed sneeze. ✓
- A payoff: it catches the butterfly — then the real one beats it with a sneeze. ✓
- A reason to rewatch: the room hides every world; the end loops into the start. ✓
- No explaining: no narration, no text needed to follow it. ✓
