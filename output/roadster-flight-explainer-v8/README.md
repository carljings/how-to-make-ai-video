# Roadster v8 — Air, Revealed

A 63.5-second Chinese text-led science film: 1080 × 1920, 30fps, 1,905 frames, no spoken narration. This revision gives the opening puzzle a visual answer and a complete musical ending.

[Film](https://github.com/carljings/how-to-make-ai-video/releases/download/production-archive-20261025/roadster-v8-answered-ending.mp4) · [中文说明](README.zh-CN.md) · [Delivery checks](QA.md) · [Verification data](out/delivery-v8/verification.json)

The opening carries one question across two shots. Air becomes visible at 4.3 seconds, while all three diagram-reading holds remain. At 55 seconds the film returns to the inverted car; the pressure arrow appears before the answer at 57.1. A softer air reveal at 59.2 lets the familiar image show its mechanism. The camera continues its orbit, decelerates, then the image fades from 62.2 to 63.4 seconds before a short black tail.

**Opus 5.5 leads the final aesthetic and pacing decisions.** It edited the final picture/sound cues, ending animation and score, and judged native rendered stills. Codex and workers engineered the initial v8 timing; Codex handles scientific boundaries, rendering, encoding and technical verification. See [creative ownership](CREATIVE_OWNERSHIP.md), [director decisions](DIRECTOR_LOCK.md), [actual collaboration](CLAUDE_COLLABORATION.md) and [production log](production-log.md).

The quiet original four-stem score has a measured PCM master of −20.0 LUFS / −6.2 dBTP, with about 0.203 seconds of trailing digital silence. These master measurements do not substitute for encoded-file checks or human listening.

The [inherited v7 science audit](SCIENCE_AUDIT.en.md) remains the factual basis; this revision is not a new factory-level verification. Geometry and motion are illustrative fits. Functional diagrams do not establish actual internal hardware, force magnitudes or flight performance. Visible cables retain an unknown role. See [sources and credits](CREDITS.md).
