# 《这首开智小曲，为什么上头？》: style preview (8.0 s, music only), creative direction

Author: Opus 5.5 (creative lead, composer, implementer). This is a style preview for the user to confirm the direction. It is not the full film.

## The direction: "踩着拍子过深渊" (Clawd crosses the void on the beat)

Clawd, the orange pixel creature from Claude Code, is built as a chunky 3D voxel toy. It crosses a black void on stepping stones. **The music lays one stone per beat, and Clawd lands on each one.** That is the rule, and the viewer sees it before any explanation.

Ghost outlines show where the next beats will land. These are predictions.

Then two beats are **withheld**: no stone and no kick, only a snare roll and a riser. Clawd crouches, its pixel seams fill with orange charge, and it leaps into the gap on faith. Half a second of near-silence follows while it hangs above a burning ghost outline. On screen: "下一拍？"

**The drop lands exactly on the predicted beat.** The stone slams into existence and the whole floor erupts out of the dark in a shock ring. Then the drop's hook is the same melody as the opening, now an octave-doubled supersaw. It pulls tiles out of the floor and **builds a giant Clawd, one row per note**. The top row and eyes lock on the final hit. The ending holds on small Clawd in front of the giant: "先让你猜中，再给你意外". The picture fades on a ringing chord and the clip ends in silence.

The mechanism the piece carries:
- a learnable rule (steady beat);
- a prediction (ghost outlines);
- a withheld event (anticipation);
- a release on the predicted time with an unpredicted size (the world transforms);
- a repeated hook that becomes the giant (familiarity).

The landing caption describes what this demo did. It is not a scientific law.

### Directions considered and rejected
- **B: Clawd as a music visualiser.** The voxels burst on every beat around a glowing logo. It is pretty but it is a screensaver: no rule, no withheld event, nothing to understand.
- **C: a marble or pinball machine.** It reads as mechanism, but it is too small-scale and too fiddly for a phone. Clawd would become a passenger.
- **A (chosen).** One causal chain that reads without words, a strong first frame, a physical transformation on the release, and multiple scales: small Clawd, the stone, a floor of about 2,000 tiles, the giant.

## Look

- **Palette.** Void black-brown (#0A0807), Claude terracotta (#D97757), ivory (#F4EBDD) for the predictions and the text, and hot orange (#FF7A3D) for energy. There is no white flash: impacts are orange heat, a screen shock ring and brief chromatic split.
- **Clawd.** The grid was measured from the official app's front pose:
  - body 8×6;
  - eyes 1×1 (unlit black, so they stay black in every light);
  - arms 2×2;
  - four legs 1×2.

  The depth is our interpretation. The body is a vinyl-toy material with faint pixel seams that glow when charged. Animation: squash and stretch, leg tuck, arm lift, two blinks.
- **Camera.** A 3/4 follow over the stones, a sink into a low upward look during the wait, an expo-out whip to the wide on the drop, and a slow settle on the two Clawds.
- **Light.** A warm key with shadows, a cool rim, light from each stone onto Clawd, and a horizon haze that rises with the drop. The bloom is restrained, and there is grain and a vignette.

## Music: original, composed in `tools/score.py`

- F minor, 120 BPM, 4 bars, about 8 s. Everything is synthesized from code (numpy):
  - kick, clap, hats, snare roll and riser;
  - offbeat pluck bass and sub plus a distorted reese bass;
  - supersaw stabs and a pad;
  - a pluck lead and a supersaw lead;
  - delay and a convolution reverb (noise IR).
- No samples, no recordings, no voice, no narration, no TTS. Nothing comes from the user's reference track or any earlier score.
- The hook is a syncopated 8-note call: F5 F5 A♭5 C6 B♭5 A♭5 F5 E♭5 on steps 0, 3, 4, 6, 8, 11, 12, 14.
- Master: about −14 LUFS and ≤ −1.2 dBTP (static gain plus a 4×-oversampled look-ahead limiter; about 1.7 dB of gain reduction at most).
- Measured section RMS levels: rule −19, withheld −25, gap −31 (the 125 ms windows inside it reach −45), drop −11, final −14 dBFS.
- **Nobody has listened to it.** These are meters only. The user will be the first listener.

## Science framing in this preview (see `SOURCES.md`)

It shows no brain, no dopamine, no numbers about people, and no claim about IQ, universal addiction or "bigger drop = more pleasure". The full film can introduce the sources.
