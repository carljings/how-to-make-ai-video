# Sources, provenance and licences: Clawd style preview

## Picture
- **Code only.** Every frame is generated by three.js and Canvas 2D code in `src/`. No images, video or textures come from outside.
- **Clawd.** Clawd is Anthropic's Claude Code mascot. Its *shape* is taken from official reference frames that Codex supplied in `.omx/artifacts/claude-music-style-preview-20261020/`:
  - `clawd-official-app-contact.jpg`, extracted from the installed Claude.app asset `ion-dist/images/install-hub/clawd-laptop.mov` (sha256 b416ac44…6f0a; see `clawd-reference-provenance.json`);
  - `clawd-official-cli-reference.png`.

  The front grid was measured from those frames at 20 px per unit: body 8×6, eyes 1×1 at body (1,1) and (6,1), arms 2×2 on body rows 2–3, legs 1×2 at body columns 0, 2, 5 and 7. The 3D depth, materials and animation are original. The reference frames are not used as footage.
- **Rejected asset.** A star or spark SVG from the first, mistaken interpretation is not used. It was moved out of the project to `.omx/artifacts/claude-music-style-preview-20261020/archive-unused/claude-symbol.svg`.
- **Font.** Noto Sans SC 500 and 900, subset to the 25 characters drawn here via Google Fonts css2 `text=` (`tools/fonts.mjs`; files and sha256 in `assets/fonts/fonts.json`). Licence: SIL OFL 1.1, in `LICENSES/Noto-Sans-SC-OFL.txt`.
- **Library.** three.js 0.186.1 (MIT), pinned in `package-lock.json`.

## Sound
- **Original composition and synthesis.** All of it is in `tools/score.py` (numpy, with ffmpeg used only to meter and encode). There are no samples, recordings, voice, narration or TTS. There is no material from the user's reference track (`reference-audio.m4a`) or from any earlier film's score.
- **Repeatable output.** Running it again regenerates `out/audio/music.wav` and `out/audio/cues.js`, the shared timing, which must be byte-identical to `src/cues.js`.
- **WAV hash differs by platform.** The WAV's sha256 can differ between machines in the last bits, because FFT and float rounding differ. Loudness and cues are identical: the M4 and the server both measured −14.1 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP, and cues.js matched.
- **No listening.** No human or model has listened to the music. All statements about it are meter readings.

## Science: what the preview does and does not say

The only captions are:
- the question "这首开智小曲 / 为什么上头？";
- "下一拍？" at the withheld beat;
- "先让你猜中，再给你意外" at the end.

The last caption describes what this demo literally did: the drop lands on the predicted beat with an unpredicted size. It is not a law. "开智" is the user's playful name for the tune, not an IQ claim.

The background the full film may draw on, with its limits:

| Idea shown | Source | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Expectation and anticipation are part of musical pleasure | Salimpoor et al. 2011, *Nature Neuroscience* 14:257–262, doi:10.1038/nn.2726 | Small sample (8 PET datasets), self-chosen music. No dopamine numbers or "magic button", and not every drop |
| Predictability (uncertainty) and surprise interact | Cheung et al. 2019, *Current Biology* 29:4084–4092, doi:10.1016/j.cub.2019.09.067 | Chord sequences, not this tune and not rhythm drops. Both low-uncertainty/high-surprise and high-uncertainty/low-surprise went with more pleasure; "more surprise is better" is not a finding |
| Repetition and familiarity can go with liking | Madison & Schiölde 2017 | Not "more repetition = always more liking", not addiction |
| Groove: easy to follow, with some variation | Senn et al. 2024 (and Witek 2014) | No universal inverted-U law; a possible factor, and it depends on the listener and context |

The picture is a conceptual demonstration, not a brain measurement. It contains no brain imagery and no dopamine traces.
